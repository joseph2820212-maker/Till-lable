# Country / regulatory profiles

**Policy (owner correction, 24 Sep 2026).** Country guidance is independent of the print engine, the data model, the
currency and the label language. A profile is optional research-backed guidance that a shop may choose to see
(for example, which fields a unit-price label in that country usually carries). TillLabel never claims that one
country's rules apply in another, and no jurisdiction-specific legal statement enters app copy without a cited
authoritative source, recorded here with its date.

The engine offers the same label kinds and fields everywhere (price, unit price and its base, conditions, validity
dates, barcode). A profile can only suggest defaults or show guidance; it never removes a field or blocks printing.

| Others (e.g. DE, FR, ES, TR, AE, SA, CA, AU) | Not started | — | Each researched and added independently |

Until a profile is sourced, the app shows no country-specific wording, and the legal disclaimer (G7) says that
shops are responsible for the pricing rules where they trade.
