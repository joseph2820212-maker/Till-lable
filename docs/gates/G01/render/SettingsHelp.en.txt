How do I contact support?
Email support@tillnote.com – the button in More → About fills in your app and device details. Add the screen you were on, what you were trying to do and screenshots if they contain no sensitive figures.
What can I safely send to support?
Your device type, operating system version, app version, the screen name, a short description and screenshots with sensitive figures hidden. Never send backup passphrases, bank details, store account passwords or unredacted product lists.
