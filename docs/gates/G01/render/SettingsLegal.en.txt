Privacy policy
This Privacy Policy explains how TillLabel, provided by LLILL LTD, handles your information. Last updated: 23 September 2026.
Offline by design
Your products, prices, label history and settings are stored only on your device. TillLabel has no account system and no server that holds your data.
No tracking
The app contains no analytics, advertising or crash-reporting libraries. Your products, prices and labels stay on your device and are not sent to TillLabel servers.
Device features the app may use
•
Camera – only to read barcodes when you open the scanner. No photo is stored.
•
Files – when you import a CSV, or export, share or back up your data.
•
Sharing – when you send a PDF or CSV to another app. What you share leaves the app and is no longer under its control.
•
In-app purchase – handled by Apple or Google and the purchase service RevenueCat, which receives a purchase receipt and an anonymous app-install identifier. No name, email or business data is sent.
•
App updates – new versions come only through the App Store or Google Play. The app does not download code updates itself.
Your responsibilities
You are responsible for the files you export or share, for your backup files and their passphrases, and for your device-level backup settings. Files you export or share leave the app and are no longer under its control.
Your control
You can edit or delete anything in the app at any time. Uninstalling the app deletes its data from the device, apart from backups and exports you made yourself.
Backups
Backup files are encrypted with a passphrase you choose. We cannot open or recover them. Where you store them is up to you.
Children
TillLabel is intended for business users and is not directed at children. LLILL LTD does not knowingly collect personal data from children.
Contacting support safely
Do not send your product lists, prices or backup files to support unless they are specifically requested and have been redacted. Never send backup passphrases, bank details or store account passwords.
Changes to this policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected in the app and the date above will be updated; the version in the app is the current one.
Privacy questions and data requests
For privacy questions or data requests, contact privacy@tillnote.com.
